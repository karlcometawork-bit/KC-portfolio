# Portfolio asset intake

Keep one portfolio in this repository. Add assets to the relevant case study and existing gallery rather than creating separate portfolios.

For each file, provide:
- Project/client and approximate date.
- Whether Karl created it, edited it, coordinated production, or supported the campaign.
- What the asset demonstrates (creative angle, tracking, reporting, workflow, result).
- For results: exact metric, period, source and whether it is team/account or individually attributable.
- Whether the client permits public portfolio use.

Useful next assets:
- PROHUB: approved launch creatives and redacted source records supporting any aggregate sales/order claim.
- Next Wave: approved ad variations, landing-page images, report visuals and non-confidential tracking examples. Keep SaaS and other acquisition workstreams distinct.
- Wingman/Ray White: public property graphics, email samples and redacted reporting examples.
- AOP: approved advertisements, captioned service videos and redacted qualification/follow-up examples.

Images: originals or high-quality PNG/JPG/WebP. Avoid screenshots that cut off dates, metric definitions or totals.
Video: original MP4 plus title, transcript/captions, project and role. Review every frame and audio before publishing.
Exclude customer records, private conversations, account IDs, billing details and material lacking publication permission.

Presentation rule: emphasize documented work with good design, clear labels and honest context. Never inflate values, imply causal uplift from a baseline, promise guaranteed results or relabel team revenue as personal achievement.
